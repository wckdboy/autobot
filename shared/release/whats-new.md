Your node's bots on your phone, a first chat in three taps, and a chat that reads like the terminal.

## Bots (Android and iOS)
- **Remote › Agents · my node lists your node's bots.** Each row shows the name, persona, model, the channels it is on (telegram · slack), and what's wrong when a channel is down. The list refreshes each time you open the tab.
- **A bot's page** shows its channels and their state, its model and approvals mode, the approvals waiting for it (you can answer them there), its recent sessions, an on/off switch, and **New chat with <bot>**.
- The agent screen and approval cards say which bot is asking. "While you were away" gives one line per bot.
- It needs Invincible 0.0.13 or newer. Older nodes keep today's single agent.

## A first chat in three taps
- **Start here** on Models picks one chat model that fits your phone. One button walks you through Download, Pause or Resume, then Start a chat.
- Autobot starts offline, and the card says so in one sentence: "Allow network and download" turns it on for the download. Once the model is ready, you can turn offline mode back on with one tap.
- **Pair a PC** and **Add an API key** on the welcome screen open what they say directly.

## Chat (Android and iOS)
- **Agent steps** read like the terminal: ▪ for each step, with what it did underneath. A failed step is red and says why.
- **Answers** have Copy, Share and Read aloud. The last one has Retry.
- **Long-press** on any message gives the same menu on both apps.

## Smaller things
- Models lists chat models first. Android shows NPU packages only on phones with a Snapdragon NPU.
- Remote, the Privacy Center and Providers use the same words on both apps. Model names replace catalog ids.
- The iOS tab bar now marks the active tab with a purple bar under its label, as on Android.
